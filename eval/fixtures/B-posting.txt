We are looking for a Marketing Communications Coordinator to support brand visibility and customer engagement from our location. This role blends creative storytelling, digital content execution, and hands-on coordination across marketing initiatives. The ideal candidate is comfortable producing high-quality written and visual materials, managing online content, and helping deliver campaigns, media outreach, and event support with consistency and attention to detail.
Responsibilities:
• Create original written and visual marketing content for digital and print use, including website copy, blog articles, advertisements, and branded imagery.
• Photograph products and refine images using design tools to develop lifestyle assets for social platforms, catalogs, and web pages.
• Maintain website content by adding new items, updating descriptions, images, and pricing, and removing products that are no longer available.
• Manage day-to-day social media activity by planning, publishing, and monitoring posts across key channels to strengthen audience engagement.
• Support email and digital marketing efforts through content preparation and campaign coordination using relevant marketing platforms.
• Prepare promotional materials such as brochures, flyers, handouts, and catalogs to support product launches and broader marketing initiatives.
• Draft press releases, media alerts, and related communications while tracking coverage and compiling performance insights for campaigns and announcements.
• Respond to customer and audience interactions across digital channels to help maintain a consistent brand voice and positive public presence.
• Coordinate logistics and marketing support for trade shows, photo shoots, and other events, including materials preparation and collaboration with vendors and internal teams.
• Work closely with sales, product development, and external partners to keep marketing projects on schedule and aligned with business goals.
• Previous experience in marketing communications, content development, social media, or a related coordination role.
• Strong copywriting and editing skills with the ability to adapt messaging for websites, email campaigns, social media, and print collateral.
• Hands-on experience with photography and image editing, including proficiency with Adobe Creative Suite and Adobe Photoshop.
• Working knowledge of Shopify, Klaviyo, Canva, Microsoft Office, and other digital marketing or content management tools.
• Familiarity with social media platform management and content scheduling, with an understanding of audience engagement best practices.
• Ability to manage multiple projects at once while maintaining accuracy, organization, and consistent follow-through.
• Customer service skills and strong communication abilities for interacting with internal teams, vendors, media contacts, and online audiences.
• Experience with QuickBooks is preferred.
